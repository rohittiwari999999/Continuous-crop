# Privacy Policy for ContinuousCrop

**Last Updated:** October 7, 2026

**ContinuousCrop** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within our mobile application.

### 1. No Data Collection
ContinuousCrop is an entirely offline, on-device photo cropping and image editing workstation.
- We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.
- We do **not** track your location, browsing habits, or device identifiers.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertisements.

### 2. Device Storage & Photos Access
The app uses the standard Android Scoped Storage API (`MediaStore.Images.Media`) solely to:
- Load the photo you select via the system Photo Picker.
- Save your cropped image outputs into your device's `Pictures/ContinuousCrop` directory.
All image processing and cropping operations are executed locally in device memory. Your photos never leave your device.

### 3. Permissions
The app requests only minimal, non-sensitive hardware access:
- `VIBRATE`: Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback when you tap "Crop & Save".

### 4. Children's Privacy
ContinuousCrop does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor does it collect any personal information from any user, including children.

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:
